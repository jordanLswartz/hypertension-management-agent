# Clinical References

These sources informed the original design. Full-text publisher PDFs are not included. Access to some sources requires a subscription, and reuse of text, figures, pathways, or screening instruments may require permission. Citations describe the research set; they do not establish that every clinical rule in the project has been validated.

## Pathways and risk equations

The architecture cites UpToDate Pathway 120405 v33.0 (initial management), 130990 v9.0 (uncontrolled monotherapy), and 130991 v8.0 (uncontrolled combination therapy). The design also references the AHA PREVENT equations and the [preventr reference implementation](https://github.com/martingmayer/preventr).

The architecture contains adaptations of clinical concepts and references to an adherence screening instrument. Publisher and instrument rights remain with their respective owners; this repository does not grant rights to their underlying materials.
